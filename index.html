Birthday Paradox Simulation

The Birthday Paradox is a phenomenon that tests how likely two people in a room share a birthday. In fact, in a group of just 23 people, the odds of two of them sharing a birthday is more than approximately 50%, which is is a lot higher than most people think. For this project, we developed a Java program to test this idea and observe how the probability of shared birthdays varies with the number of people in the group.

Our program follows the idea of the Birthday Paradox by creating groups of people and generating a random birthday for each person to check if there are any matching birthdays. The program repeats this process several times for different group sizes and calculates the number of times a matching birthday is found.
